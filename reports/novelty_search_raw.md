## Closest prior art (top 5) and honest assessment

1. Chapagai et al. 2026 (arXiv 2611.05011): explanation-derived feature + margin + TTA disagreement into logistic regression to predict classifier errors; AURC; multi-site shift. Overlaps steps 3-5 structurally. Their result is negative (explanation adds <=0.011 AUROC_f).
2. Kalita and Singh 2026 (arXiv 2610.36640): perturbation consistency/flip stability as an error detector; shows it underperforms MSP on clean data. Overlaps the "flip rate/stability" features.
3. DEGRE (AAAI) and ConfidNet (NeurIPS 2019): learned meta-detector on confidence plus ensemble disagreement, medical imaging, AURC. Overlaps the learned-detector and non-explanation features.
4. Lei et al. 2024 (arXiv 2401.03664): BUS-specific attribution (RISE-type) plus uncertainty reliability score on BUSI.
5. Thomas et al. 2026 (arXiv 2610.41763) with Lenis et al. (arXiv 2007.06312) and Gurram 2026 (arXiv 2611.13006): local-blur foreground vs background sensitivity ratio, healthy-tissue region replacement, lesion vs same-shape control erasure. These cover the perturbation toolkit pieces.
(Also Ye and Durrett 2021 for the general idea in text; Ma et al. 2026 for learned failure detector in cancer imaging.)

What is NOT novel: region-wise occlusion/blur, area-matched controls, perturbation flip rates, learned correctness predictors, ensemble/entropy features, AURC/risk-coverage, cross-dataset BUS shift, Grad-CAM on BUSI/BUS-BRA.

What could still be novel (none verified as absent; I searched but cannot prove a negative):
- Algorithm (weakest claim): the specific combination of predicted-mask-derived lesion/ring/background counterfactual sensitivities as features for an error detector. I did not find a paper doing exactly this, but components exist separately (items 1, 2, 5). Honest risk: reviewers will frame it as Chapagai+Kalita applied to BUS with region masks.
- Workflow: using only predicted (not ground-truth) masks, OOF training, and a fixed-alert-rate recall of false-negative malignant cases is a deployment-realistic protocol; I found no BUS paper with that protocol, but this is a workflow/evaluation contribution, not a new algorithm. Note Traub 2024 criticises AURC alone, so add AUGRC and AUROC_f/Error-AUPRC; and Jaeger 2023 / Bernhardt 2022 imply MSP and logit margin must be the primary baselines.
- Empirical finding (most defensible): whether region-specific counterfactual sensitivity adds information beyond margin/entropy/ensemble for BUS, and whether it transfers BUS-BRA to BUSI. Given the two negative results above (Chapagai, Kalita), either outcome is publishable if tested with strong baselines and ablations (confidence-only vs +sensitivity, dedup BUSI, patient-level splits). A negative result would match the literature; a positive one would be the stronger novelty.
- Validity risk specific to your perturbations: Poolpol 2026 and Rong 2022 show fixed-value/black/blur replacement creates OOD artefacts that can drive logit changes; your random and area-matched controls address this only if used to normalise (report sensitivity minus control).
- Search limits: standard search plus one extended search; some searches could not confirm absence (e.g. unpublished or non-indexed BUS conference papers, journal pages blocked: Medical Physics for BUS-BRA, PMC for Zech).
